# Training-log examples: not test results

> **Warning.** Every image in this folder comes from logs 1, 2 and 4, whose human frames were used to train M5.
> They illustrate what a given per-knot IoU looks like; they are not test results and must not be quoted as M5
> performance. Test results are on held-out log 10 only (`../heldout_log10/`).

These 18 knots fill the IoU bins (mainly thin knots at 0.1, 0.3 and 0.9) where log 10 has no example. Captions,
IoU values and knot positions are in [index.json](index.json); each caption repeats the warning.
